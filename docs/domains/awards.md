# Awards

::: hcsim.engine.awards

## Data findings

- A replay that routes history's recorded awards reproduces the reference ledger's monthly SBC
  to a monthly WAPE of 1.3% over a closed six-month window of the tiny world, and of 3.0% over a
  year of the small world.
- Grants the run sizes itself run large: sized new-hire grants average about five times the
  shares of history's recorded awards on the tiny world (issue #633).
