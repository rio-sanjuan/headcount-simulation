# Cancellation

::: hcsim.engine.domains.cancellation

## Data findings

- Unfilled exit hazards by requisition age recover the world's: 0.008, 0.016, 0.035, and 0.073
  at ages 0, 1, 2, and 3 or more months, against the truth's 0.01, 0.02, 0.04, and 0.08 on the
  medium world.
- A boosted model beats the null arm on the medium world (log loss 0.1323 to 0.1097). On the
  small world there's too little signal, and the null arm stays.
