# Backfill

::: hcsim.engine.domains.backfill

## Data findings

- The pooled backfill rate recovers the world's: 0.624 against the truth's 0.627 on the medium
  world.
- The truth's backfill probability falls with level, from 0.66 at level 1 to 0.42 at level 7.
  The backfill arms read only the cost center, so the champion is flat and misses that shift; a
  level-aware arm would close it.
